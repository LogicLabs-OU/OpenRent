Electrical safety tests (PAT): service intervals of the kind electrical test say which units need one and how often. Tests are recorded on the unit's page with their readings, kept unchanged, and printed as certificates. A failed test takes the unit out of service with a repair ticket, and a unit without a valid test cannot be scanned onto a booking or picked up.
