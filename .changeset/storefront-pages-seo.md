Storefront pages with sections per theme, header and footer menus, built-in locations and contact form sections, page titles, Open Graph tags, structured data, a sitemap and robots.txt (theme API 1.9). The SDK gains `sitemap()` and `page(slug)`; the starter has page descriptions, canonical links, share tags, `/sitemap.xml` and `/robots.txt`.
